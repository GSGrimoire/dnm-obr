Version 1.16

Playing on a token in Owlbear Rodeo now works reliably, and the creator no longer needs anything from the internet to run.

  • Fixed: opening a character from a token could quietly fall back to the ordinary creator, showing the import screen instead of your character. Anything you changed was saved to your browser rather than to the token, and your rolls and Momentum never reached the table.
  • Fixed: rolls made from the sheet were reaching the shared log without their Difficulty, so they always read as if you had rolled against Difficulty 0.
  • The creator is now completely self-contained. It loads nothing from anywhere, which means it also works offline and from a saved copy on your own machine.
